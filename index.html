01

Understanding ProRes vs Apple Log

Two separate things. They do different jobs.

Codec

ProRes

The Quality of the Container

Normal (HEVC)

~350 MB/min. Push colors and it falls apart.

Think JPEG

ProRes

~6 GB/min. Grade holds up — smooth gradients, clean detail.

Think RAW

Tradeoff: Massive files. Requires external USB-C SSD.

Color Profile

Apple Log

How Light and Color Are Recorded

Standard

Processed in real time. Looks great but baked in. Blown sky = gone.

Apple Log

Flat grey image on purpose. All detail preserved. Magic in post.

Analogy

Standard = Cooked meal. Apple Log = Raw ingredients.

How They Combine

CodecProfileSizeFlexBest For
HEVCStandard~350 MB/minLowQuick casual
HEVCApple LogSmall–medHighSocial & web
ProResApple Log~6 GB/minMaxCinema, festivals
ProResHDRHugeMedHQ w/o grading

02

Recommended Recording Format

The Sweet Spot

HEVC + Apple Log

For social media and website videos

01

Platforms recompress on upload. HEVC Log vs ProRes Log = zero difference.

02

10–15x smaller files than ProRes.

03

Editing stays fast — files aren't massive.

04

Full flat Log with complete grading flexibility.

05

No external SSD required.

When to consider ProRes

  • Film festival / broadcast
  • Desktop editing (DaVinci Resolve)
  • Heavy compositing / VFX
  • Large displays

05

Composition & Framing

Rule of Thirds

Subject on intersection points, not center.

Leading Lines

Roads, fences, rivers → guide the eye.

Foreground Depth

Shoot through doorways, branches → 3D feel.

Symmetry

Center framing = tension. Architecture, tunnels.

Negative Space

Empty space = scale, isolation, calm.

Vary Shots

Wide, medium, close-up, detail, angles. Edit needs options.

Next: Lighting
06

Lighting Tips

Golden Hour

#1 factor. First/last hour of sun. Warm, soft, directional.

Blue Hour

20–30 min pre-sunrise / post-sunset. Cool, moody.

Backlighting

Light behind subject = rim light + separation.

Overcast

Natural softbox. Smooth skin, gentle shadows.

Avoid Harsh Midday

Hard shadows, blown highlights. Find shade.

Expose for highlights. Shadows recoverable. Blown highlights are not.

LUTs What & How

A .cube file that remaps every color in your footage.

Technical

Conversion LUT

Log → Rec.709. Develops the flat image.

Creative

Creative LUT

After conversion. Mood — warm, teal & orange, vintage.

Sources

Phantom LUTs

~$30–50

ARRI calibrated. DJI matching.

FilterGrade

Free

8 .cube files, VN compatible.

IWLTBAP

~$40

325+ cinematic LUTs.

LUT Studio

Free/Sub

90+ built-in, Apple Log.

Best Practices

Color correct FIRST before creative LUT
Conversion LUT first, creative on top
Never 100% — dial back for natural look
Same LUT across all clips for consistency
